3D Surface Lab

Conicoids (replace the plot):
xto yto zto uto vto

Parametric surface: type x(u,v); y(u,v); z(u,v) in a row and set the u, v ranges. The conicoid buttons use this, which is why they come out smooth.

Domain and z-range accept numbers or expressions like 2*pi. Leave z blank for auto; if you set it, parts of a surface outside the range are cut off. Drag to rotate, pinch or scroll to zoom.
